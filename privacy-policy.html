Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction & Commitment

Welcome to Jungle Safari Tours Uttarakhand ("we", "our", "us"). We operate authorized wildlife jungle safaris and permit booking services in Rajaji National Park and adjacent conservation reserves in Uttarakhand, India. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

2. Information We Collect

When you inquire about or book a jungle safari with us via website forms, telephone, or WhatsApp, we collect:

  • Contact Details: Full name, mobile number, email address, and city of residence.
  • Safari Requirements: Selected safari zone/gate (Chilla, Ranipur, Jhilmil Jhel, Chillawali Mohand, Vindhyawasini, Motichur), preferred date, shift timing (Morning/Evening), and number of adults/children.
  • Government Permit Documentation: As mandated by the Uttarakhand Forest Department and Wildlife Protection Act, valid government photo ID details (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driver's License, or International Passport/Visa copies for foreign nationals) are required to issue official entry permits.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Securing official Forest Department gate permits and paying statutory government entry and road taxes.
  • Assigning registered 4x4 open safari Gypsies (UK14TB0802 & UK14TB7866) and certified local naturalist guides (Rehman & Jishan Rehman).
  • Sending booking confirmation receipts, reporting times, meeting location maps, and safari preparation guidelines.
  • Customer support and responding to inquiries via telephone or WhatsApp.

4. Data Protection & Non-Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any commercial third-party marketing companies. Your details are only shared with the official Uttarakhand Forest Department counters for statutory permit issuance and with your assigned safari driver for gate reception.

5. Cookies & Advertising Analytics

Our website uses standard technical cookies and analytics tools (including Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion tags) to analyze website traffic, optimize page speed, and evaluate advertisement performance. These tools do not collect personally identifiable banking or passport data. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
