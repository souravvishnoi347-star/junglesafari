What to Know Before You Book

Wildlife safari permits are issued by the Forest Department of Uttarakhand under stringent visitor limits to preserve biodiversity. Here is our promise to you:

📄
Original ID Mandatory
Every guest must carry the same government ID (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport) used at the time of permit reservation.
🛡️
Zero Hidden Fees
What we quote covers vehicle, driver, government forest permits, road tax, and entry tickets.
🔄
Flexible Rescheduling
Date modification assistance provided subject to forest department slot availability.
⭐
Experienced Drivers
Only authorized 4x4 Gypsies with veteran drivers skilled in jungle navigation and animal etiquette.
