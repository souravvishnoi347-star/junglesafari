Safari Zones

Explore Rajaji Tiger Reserve Safari Zones

Experience wild elephant herds, tigers, leopards, and over 315 bird species across the premier safari zones of Rajaji National Park.

Gate 1 • Chilla Range

Chilla Safari Zone

The flagship safari range of Rajaji Tiger Reserve, located along the eastern bank of the holy Ganges near Haridwar. Sprawling over 148 sq km, Chilla is celebrated for open grasslands, 36 km of scenic game tracks, frequent sightings of wild Asian elephant herds, leopards, tigers, and spotted deer.

📍 Haridwar / Chilla Dam Gate 🕐 Nov–June 🐘 Elephant & Tiger Sighting
Explore Chilla Zone →
Gate 2 • Ranipur Haridwar

Ranipur Zone Haridwar

Situated directly at the southwestern fringe of Haridwar near the Shivalik foothills and BHEL. Renowned for rugged hill tracks, seasonal streams (raus), dry deciduous forests, high leopard sighting density, barking deer, sambar, and over 250 species of rare woodland birds.

📍 Haridwar / Ranipur Gate 🕐 Nov–June 🐆 Leopard & Birding Trails
Explore Ranipur Zone →
Gate 3 • Wetland Sanctuary

Jhilmil Jhel Zone

A world-famous wetland and marshland conservation reserve situated just 15 km from Haridwar near Tantwala. Jhilmil Jhel is the exclusive habitat in Uttarakhand home to the rare Swamp Deer (Barasingha), wild elephants, marsh leopards, and migratory winter waterfowl.

📍 Haridwar / Tantwala Gate 🕐 Nov–June 🦌 Swamp Deer (Barasingha)
Explore Jhilmil Jhel →
Gate 4 • Mohand Pass

Chillawali Mohand Zone

Located along the historic Mohand Pass forest corridor on the Shivalik hills connecting Dehradun and Saharanpur. Characterized by dense, towering sal canopies, deep rocky ravines, and authentic wilderness sightings of elephants, sloth bears, and striped hyenas.

📍 Mohand / Shivalik Pass Gate 🕐 Nov–June 🌲 Deep Sal Forest & Bears
Explore Chillawali Mohand →
Gate 5 • Eastern Shivalik

Vindhyawasini Zone

Set in the pristine Rawasan river valley near the sacred Vindhyawasini forest belt. This untamed zone features dramatic Shivalik ridge backdrops, riverbed crossings, goral mountain goats, leopards, and secluded elephant herds far from commercial tourist trails.

📍 Rawasan River Valley Gate 🕐 Nov–June ⛰️ Scenic Ridge & Goral Trails
Explore Vindhyawasini →
Gate 6 • Raiwala Corridor

Motichur Safari Zone

Positioned on the western bank of the Ganges near Raiwala along the Haridwar–Rishikesh highway. Motichur boasts ancient sal forests, winding gravel streams (raus), vital elephant migratory corridor trails, and exceptional territory for stealthy Indian leopards.

📍 Raiwala / Haridwar–Rishikesh 🕐 Nov–June 🐾 Ancient Sal & Leopard Habitat
Explore Motichur Zone →

Frequently Asked Questions

The best time for a jungle safari in Uttarakhand is from November to June. The winter months (November–February) offer excellent wildlife sightings as animals gather near water sources, and the cool weather makes the experience pleasant. Summer months (March–June) are ideal for tiger and elephant sightings since the sparse vegetation improves visibility significantly. Rajaji National Park remains open during this period, though specific zones may have varying schedules. The park is generally closed during the monsoon season (July–September) due to heavy rainfall, flooding, and to allow wildlife breeding and habitat regeneration.

You can book a safari in Rajaji National Park through our website or by contacting us directly via phone or WhatsApp. Online bookings can also be made through the official Uttarakhand Forest Department portal. You will need to provide a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, or Driving License) for all visitors. We recommend booking at least 2–3 days in advance, especially during weekends and holidays, as permits are limited per zone per day. Our team handles all permit formalities, park entry fees, guide arrangements, and vehicle booking so you can enjoy a completely hassle-free wildlife experience.

Rajaji National Park safaris are conducted in government-registered 4x4 open Gypsies accommodating up to 6 persons. Packages arranged by Jishan Rehman and Rehman include official Uttarakhand Forest Department gate permits, certified local naturalist guide, experienced driver, and all applicable road taxes. Contact us via WhatsApp or phone for immediate permit slot availability and custom package quotes.

Rajaji National Park and its surrounding protected sanctuaries feature 6 official safari gates and zones: 1) Chilla Zone (premier wildlife zone along the Ganges, renowned for wild elephant herds and tigers); 2) Ranipur Zone Haridwar (rugged foothills near BHEL with high leopard sightings and birds); 3) Jhilmil Jhel Zone (exclusive wetland habitat of the endangered Swamp Deer / Barasingha); 4) Chillawali Mohand Zone (towering sal forests and elephant corridors along Mohand Pass); 5) Vindhyawasini Zone (scenic valley and goral mountain goat trails); and 6) Motichur Zone (ancient sal forest and vital wildlife corridor near Raiwala). For first-timers, Chilla morning safari is the most popular, while Ranipur and Motichur are exceptional for tranquil predator tracking.

Rajaji National Park is home to an extraordinary diversity of wildlife. The park's star attractions include herds of Asian Elephants (over 600 individuals), Royal Bengal Tigers, Indian Leopards, Himalayan Black Bears, Sloth Bears, and Spotted Deer (Chital). You may also encounter Sambar Deer, Barking Deer (Muntjac), Wild Boar, Indian Pangolin, King Cobra, Monitor Lizards, and Gharials in the river sections. The park is a birdwatcher's paradise with over 400 documented species including the Great Pied Hornbill, Crested Serpent Eagle, Pallas's Fish Eagle, and various species of woodpeckers, kingfishers, and flycatchers. The Shivalik hills and Ganges riverbed create diverse microhabitats supporting this remarkably rich biodiversity.

No, Rajaji National Park is generally closed during the monsoon season from July 1st to October 15th (dates may vary slightly each year as per forest department notifications). The closure is necessary to allow wildlife breeding, habitat regeneration, and because heavy monsoon rainfall makes forest roads unsafe and impassable. The Ganges and Song rivers that flow through the park swell significantly during this period. We strongly recommend planning your visit between November and June for the best wildlife sighting opportunities and safest conditions.

Rajaji National Park is conveniently located approximately 220 km from Delhi, making it one of the most accessible national parks from the capital. By road, you can drive via NH-58 (Delhi–Meerut Expressway to Haridwar) in approximately 4.5–5 hours. The nearest railway station is Haridwar Junction (just 5 km from the Chilla gate), with frequent trains from Delhi including the Shatabdi Express, Jan Shatabdi Express, and Mussoorie Express. The nearest airport is Jolly Grant Airport in Dehradun (about 35 km away), connected to Delhi by daily flights on major airlines. Our safari packages include complimentary pick-up and drop from Haridwar railway station, Dehradun airport, or your hotel in the region.

The 6 official safari gates and zones operate in the following official order: 1. Chilla Zone (east bank of the Ganges near Haridwar/Rishikesh), 2. Ranipur Zone Haridwar (southwestern edge near BHEL foothills), 3. Jhilmil Jhel Zone (famed wetland sanctuary 15 km from Haridwar), 4. Chillawali Mohand Zone (dense sal forest pass connecting Dehradun/Saharanpur), 5. Vindhyawasini Zone (Rawasan river valley and eastern Shivalik buffer), and 6. Motichur Zone (vital elephant migratory corridor near Raiwala). Each zone operates with controlled daily vehicle permits issued by the Uttarakhand Forest Department.
