Our Roots

Dedicated to the Wild Spirit of Uttarakhand

Founded in Haridwar by native naturalists and passionate conservationists, Jungle Safari Tours Uttarakhand began with a clear mission: to connect travelers with the majesty of wild nature while strictly upholding the conservation guidelines of the Uttarakhand Forest Department.

Over the last 12+ years, we have hosted over 50,000 satisfied wildlife travelers, wildlife photographers, conservation researchers, and families from around the globe. Our drivers are born and raised around the borders of Rajaji National Park — they understand animal calls, alarm sounds, pugmarks, and seasonal migration paths better than anyone.

We manage every step of your safari journey: from forest department paperwork, vehicle permits, and certified guides to luxury resort accommodations and private transfers.
